# Hospital-Management-Business-Intelligence-Dashboard-Suite
– Designed and developed an end-to-end Tableau Business Intelligence solution analyzing 10,750+ healthcare records across admissions, operations, clinical quality, financial performance, and patient experience. 
– Built 5 interactive dashboards containing 25+ visualizations and KPI scorecards for hospital executives, department heads, and operational stakeholders. 
– Developed key healthcare and business metrics including Bed Occupancy Rate, Readmission Rate, Mortality Rate, Average Length of Stay, Revenue Growth, Gross Profit Margin, Appointment Completion Rate, Patient Wait Time, and Laboratory Turnaround Time. 
– Performed healthcare operations analysis to identify staffing shortages, resource utilization gaps, patient flow bottlenecks, appointment inefficiencies, and revenue optimization opportunities. – Implemented dashboard navigation, drill-down reporting, filter actions, parameter controls, and cross-dashboard analytical workflows to support data-driven decision making.
– Generated actionable business recommendations that improved visibility into hospital performance, resource planning, operational efficiency, and patient experience management.
