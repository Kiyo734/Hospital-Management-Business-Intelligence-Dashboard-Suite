## 🏥 Healthcare Business Intelligence Dashboard - Tableau

* Designed and developed an end-to-end **Tableau Business Intelligence solution** analyzing **10,750+ healthcare records** across admissions, operations, clinical quality, financial performance, and patient experience.
* Built **5 interactive dashboards** with **25+ visualizations and KPI scorecards** for hospital executives, department heads, and operational stakeholders.
* Developed key healthcare metrics including **Bed Occupancy Rate, Readmission Rate, Mortality Rate, Average Length of Stay, Revenue Growth, Gross Profit Margin, Appointment Completion Rate, Patient Wait Time, and Laboratory Turnaround Time**.
* Performed healthcare operations analysis to identify **staffing shortages, resource utilization gaps, patient flow bottlenecks, appointment inefficiencies, and revenue optimization opportunities**.
* Implemented **dashboard navigation, drill-down reporting, filter actions, parameter controls, and cross-dashboard analytical workflows** to support data-driven decision-making.
* Generated actionable insights to improve **hospital performance visibility, resource planning, operational efficiency, and patient experience management**.
